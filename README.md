# eclipsereader

A manga-first reader for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Manga reads right to left with real page turns,
manhwa and webtoons scroll as one seamless strip, and everything stays on your device.

## Get the app

1. Open the **Actions** tab, then the newest green **Build eclipsereader IPA** run.
2. Download **EclipseReader-unsigned-IPA** at the bottom of the run, unzip it and sideload the `.ipa`.
3. The **screenshots** artifact from the same run has every screen photographed on a small and a large iPhone,
   iPad, iPad mini and the Duo, in portrait and landscape.

Every push to `main` builds automatically. The source is in `EclipseReader/`.

## What's inside

- **Library**: cover grid (three sizes), unread, new and downloaded badges, continue-reading card, filters and sorting.
- **Reader**: paper-curl or slide page turns, two-page spreads, tap the page edges to turn, chapter skip, a page slider that follows the reading direction, warm night filter, crop margins, tap-to-translate.
- **Manhwa and webtoons**: detected automatically, with seamless vertical strips.
- **Browse**: built-in browser with ad and app-banner blocking and one-tap *Add to library*. Native connections to MangaDex (search and popular titles), WeebCentral, WEBTOON and ReadBerserk, plus Asura Scans, MangaDot, MangaBall, Comix and official publishers.
- **Downloads** for offline reading, **Updates** for new chapters, **History**, backup and restore.
- **Files**: import CBZ, ZIP, PDF, images or folders.

Not affiliated with any site it can open. No manga is bundled.
